About Me

Learning, practice and an interest in the digital business landscape.

MOHAMMAD RAFIQ SIDDIQI

I am a Digital Marketing & E-Commerce professional from Afghanistan, with a strong interest in digital business, marketing strategy, brand development, and online growth. I am interested in how businesses can use digital platforms, marketing strategies, and data to build stronger brands, connect with their audiences, and grow online.

I have developed a foundation in digital marketing and e-commerce and continue to strengthen my knowledge through learning, practical projects, and real-world campaign experience. My areas of interest include digital advertising, social media marketing, content strategy, audience research, campaign planning, and digital business development. I aim to combine strategic thinking, creativity, and practical execution to help businesses achieve their marketing goals.

I completed a six-month Professional Digital Marketing & E-Commerce training program under the UNICEF Skills and Livelihood Program, implemented by NETLINKS Ltd. This training helped me develop an understanding of digital marketing principles, industry tools, marketing strategies, and the practical skills needed to continue building my professional career.

I am committed to continuous learning, practical experience, and professional development, with a long-term goal of growing into a skilled digital marketing professional and contributing to meaningful business growth through digital solutions.
